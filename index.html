What is the minimum order quantity for wholesale pricing? +
We cater primarily to B2B and institutional clients. Wholesale pricing applies to carton-level orders or scheduled monthly replenishment contracts. Contact our desk for custom institutional quotations.
Do you offer free delivery across Vijayawada? +
Yes! We provide free express doorstep delivery across all major commercial and institutional zones in Vijayawada, Ramachandra Nagar, and surrounding areas.
How quickly are quotations processed? +
Quotations submitted through our digital desk or WhatsApp are processed instantly and delivered within 30 minutes during standard business hours (Mon-Sat, 9 AM - 7 PM).

Absolutely. Many corporate offices and hospitals partner with us for scheduled monthly restocking of copier paper, files, and hygiene consumables to ensure zero stockouts.
